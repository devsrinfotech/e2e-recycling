About e2e Corporate Recycling

e2e is a KSPCB-registered recycler supporting corporate IT, procurement, facilities, and compliance teams. With 20 years of e-waste experience and 15 years of Pan-India logistics expertise, we design practical retirement programs around each organisation's equipment, security, and reporting needs.

Our verified services include Pan-India collection and logistics, Bengaluru, Chennai, and Hyderabad coverage, data sanitization, physical drive destruction, prototype destruction, and onsite assessments. The result is secure handling, responsible recycling, and clearer compliance documentation.

Why Corporate E-Waste Management Matters in India

For Indian businesses, retiring laptops, servers, networking equipment, and prototypes requires more than a pickup. A controlled process helps IT and compliance teams protect data, document asset movement, and route equipment through responsible recycling. e2e supports onsite assessments, secure destruction, Pan-India logistics, and compliant disposal, with environmental outcomes as a result of good corporate controls.
