Secure, documented e-waste and ITAD outcomes for corporate teams

KSPCB-registered e2e supports IT, procurement, facilities, compliance, and sustainability teams with 20 years of e-waste experience and 15 years of Pan-India logistics expertise, plus 100% data destruction.

Comprehensive Electronics Recycling

e2e provides comprehensive recycling for corporate electronics and retired IT assets, helping businesses recover value, reduce environmental risk, and meet applicable e-waste requirements. Our documented process supports responsible downstream handling and certification.

EPR Planning and Implementation

e2e helps producers and corporate stakeholders plan and implement EPR programs with practical collection, recycling, documentation, and certification support aligned to compliance objectives.

Pan-India Logistic Support

Our verified Pan-India collection and logistics capability supports distributed corporate rollouts, with coverage across Bengaluru, Chennai, and Hyderabad. We coordinate collection, transport, secure handling, and compliant downstream processing.

E-waste Collection, Storage, and Recycling

e2e manages collection, secure storage, sorting, and recycling for retired equipment so corporate teams have one accountable service path, clearer records, and responsible material recovery.

Prototype Destruction

Protect confidential designs and intellectual property with secure prototype destruction for development, testing, and product teams. e2e provides controlled handling and documentation for prototypes that must not re-enter circulation.

Data Sanitization

e2e delivers data sanitization with 100% data destruction for retired laptops, servers, and storage devices, including physical drive destruction when required. We also conduct onsite assessments to define scope, risk controls, and documentation for secure retirement.
