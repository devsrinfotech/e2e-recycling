Corporate e-waste questions, answered

e2e is a KSPCB-registered recycler supporting IT, procurement, facilities, compliance, and sustainability teams with secure asset disposition and responsible recycling across India.

We accept corporate IT and electronic assets such as laptops, desktops, servers, networking equipment, monitors, printers, mobile devices, storage media, batteries, circuit boards, and related equipment. Share your asset list during assessment so we can confirm handling requirements and route recoverable materials responsibly.

Data sanitization removes data from a reusable drive through a controlled erasure process. Physical drive destruction makes the storage media unreadable by physically destroying it. We recommend the route based on your security policy, asset condition, and reuse decision, and can provide 100% data destruction with documentation and certification.

Yes. We can plan controlled destruction of prototypes, development hardware, and other sensitive assets, including physical destruction where required. We document the agreed scope and route materials into responsible recycling and recovery after destruction.

Yes. We provide Pan-India collection and logistics coordination for single-site and multi-location corporate programs. We have coverage in Bengaluru, Chennai, and Hyderabad, and can align pickup planning, asset movement, and reporting across locations.

Yes. Onsite assessments help teams review asset types, volumes, storage areas, access constraints, data-bearing equipment, and documentation needs before collection. We use the findings to shape a practical, controlled plan for pickup, destruction, recycling, and reporting.

Timing depends on location, volume, asset type, access, and scheduling. Same-day pickup is possible in Bengaluru only, subject to location, volume, and scheduling. For Chennai, Hyderabad, and other Pan-India locations, we coordinate a suitable collection window with your team.

Assets are received, recorded, sorted, and routed according to the agreed plan. Data-bearing equipment follows the selected sanitization or destruction route, while reusable components and materials are recovered where appropriate. Hazardous fractions are handled through responsible recycling channels to reduce landfill impact.

e2e provides comprehensive documentation, including certificates of recycling and data destruction, for your records and compliance audits. The agreed reporting package can cover collected assets, data destruction, recycling outcomes, and compliance documentation.

We help corporate teams plan documented collection, responsible recycling, data destruction, and reporting aligned to their EPR and internal compliance workflows. As a KSPCB-registered recycler, e2e supports evidence gathering for audits while responsible recovery helps conserve resources and reduce landfill waste.

Use our corporate assessment request and share your locations, approximate asset categories and volumes, preferred timing, access requirements, data-bearing assets, and reporting needs. Keep assets segregated where practical, identify prototypes or drives requiring destruction, and confirm an authorised contact for the assessment.
