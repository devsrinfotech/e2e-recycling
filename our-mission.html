Responsible recycling is part of operational risk management

The environmental context matters because business decisions shape the outcome.
  • India generates substantial volumes of e-waste each year, and unsafe handling in the unorganised sector creates environmental and health risks.
  • Corporate disposal needs a controlled, documented route from collection to final processing.
A business response should be practical, measurable, and responsible.
  • Data-bearing assets need secure sanitization or physical destruction before recycling.
  • Recoverable materials should be routed back into responsible recycling streams instead of landfill.
  • Hazardous materials in e-waste (such as lead, mercury, and cadmium) pose a serious risk to public health and the environment if not handled properly.
