Server, workstation and laptop retirement under IT control

For Hyderabad projects involving workstations, servers and laptop fleets, map storage media to the equipment list before requesting disposal. Keep production shutdown and data release approval within your IT change process. Use separate batches when server media and office peripherals have different readiness dates.

  • Include drive counts as well as server chassis counts.
  • Tell us whether data-bearing assets may leave the premises.
  • Confirm access approvals, collection batches and who accepts the completion records.

Collection timing and service location

Hyderabad pickup and any onsite feasibility assessment are planned around your site and security requirements. Collection timing and sanitization completion are separate milestones.

e2e is based in Tumkur, Karnataka. This page describes service coverage in Hyderabad; it does not represent a local branch or processing facility. Ask for the collection, transit and processing arrangements for your project.

What to include in your enquiry

Send the pickup city and postcode, company contact, approximate counts by equipment type, data-bearing drive counts, target dates and access limitations. For more than one office, include a site code and contact for each location. Use the enquiry brief builder to structure the scope.

Before handover, request current registration evidence and agree the inventory, custody records, sanitization outcomes and recycling documentation required by your company. Your IT team should approve the release of data-bearing assets.

Questions from corporate IT teams

Do you collect corporate e-waste in Hyderabad?

Yes. e2e serves Hyderabad through its collection and logistics service. Share asset types, quantities, addresses, data requirements and a preferred window so the scope can be confirmed.

Can data erasure be requested separately?

Yes. Specify whether the equipment is being retained, returned or recycled. Media compatibility, software, execution location and reporting are confirmed after assessment.
